Hey everyone!

It's been a little while! Here's a catch-up on the past three-ish months.

Family

It's been almost seven years since Suvdaa and I met. Truly amazing how time flies. These are some photos from our early days, just a couple of years in.

Life has been full since. Our two kiddos are growing up fast, and I've been trying to be present in the moment as much as I can. We've gone camping a lot this year. The boys are older, and being on the road is much more manageable now - starting with not having to bring diapers everywhere.

I've also been getting into a good rhythm of smoking meat - pretty much any kind - and making a decent Korean BBQ.

Professional

I've been building nonstop:

  • Kedge - an open-source document review tool with inline comments, suggested edits, and AI reviewers. I've been using this one a lot myself.
  • Groove - a simple workout app built around small sets spread throughout the day. Pick a few movements, fit them around your routine, and build the habit of staying active daily.
  • Otor - an early plant-coaching project. I'm working on monitoring soil moisture, light, temperature, and humidity, with the goal of turning those readings into practical advice on what each plant needs.

I'm still super pleased with how well Plotling is holding up. It turns everyday family moments into illustrated stories featuring people your kids actually know. Just made this little book for the kiddos. Honestly, give it a try if you have a little one! I'm also open to collaborating if you have ideas for expanding the app or getting it out to more people.

I've also been dabbling more and more with chips and IoT projects. You might recognize some of the sensors and chips in here.

Trading

Tough trading environment. That's all I can say. It feels like individual stocks and some sectors would love to run, but the overall macro picture and uncertainty around the war and oil are keeping people on edge.

Travel & Adventures

Anyways, we've managed to get out on multiple camping trips over the summer. The PNW is just gorgeous this time of year, and everything feels so accessible.

The Snow Peak campground and a cabin trip near Mt. Baker stand out. Maybe for the convenience, or maybe just for the good company :)

I also made a surprise visit to Chicago to meet up with Abi and Tukker's fam. Seeing the kids grow up is insane. Some are already wearing bigger shoes than us! Sadly, I don't have many pictures. We mostly stayed indoors, hung out, drank, and watched movies. A good ol' hang.

I tried making khorkhog - actual Mongolian BBQ - but failed pretty badly. The meat came out super dry and wasn't seasoned well. Well, I'll try to do better next time.

Everything I do these days is about keeping these two as happy as they can be. Watching them grow up together, play all day (and, obviously, fight all day too) just melts me.

They're growing up in the cul-de-sac, playing with their friends every day without a worry in the world. Meanwhile, we parents get to enjoy each other's company and sneak a beer here and there.

I know we're living some of the best moments of our lives. The thought that one day we'll look back on all of this as memories makes me a little sad. For now, I'm trying to be present and soak it all up.
