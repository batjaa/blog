## Professional

I've been building nonstop:

- **[Kedge](https://kedge.page/)** - an open-source document review tool with inline comments, suggested edits, and AI reviewers. I've been using this one a lot myself.
- **[Groove](https://usegroove.app)** - a simple workout app built around small sets spread throughout the day. Pick a few movements, fit them around your routine, and build the habit of staying active daily.
- **[Otor](https://otor.preview.batjaa.site)** - an early plant-coaching project. I'm working on monitoring soil moisture, light, temperature, and humidity, with the goal of turning those readings into practical advice on what each plant needs.

I'm still super pleased with how well **[Plotling](https://plotling.app)** is holding up. It turns everyday family moments into illustrated stories featuring people your kids actually know. Just made [this little book for the kiddos](https://plotling.app/s/rHKF5qFGsAga80OgcY6iLYm6FVR6dgeJ). Honestly, give it a try if you have a little one! I'm also open to collaborating if you have ideas for expanding the app or getting it out to more people.
